# Portfolio review checklist

Before sharing this case study:

- Verify your individual role/contribution wording and disclose relevant AI assistance accurately. Do not imply sole authorship of all implementation/research.
- State the problem, intended audience, actual local scope, cost constraints and deferred online functionality.
- Show alternatives and the rationale for the candle metaphor, hierarchy, map density, equivalent access, optional fields and recovery states.
- Link decisions to evidence and golden requirements; show a real correction rather than presenting an unrealistically perfect process.
- Use actual screenshots with viewport/commit/state provenance; label samples and distinguish a design version from a current screen.
- Keep participant research marked NOT RUN until it is genuinely performed. Do not turn owner feedback into a user-study sample.
- State accessibility/performance evidence precisely and keep manual gaps visible; no full WCAG/ISO certificate or invented Core Web Vitals scores.
- Report measured outcomes only if data exists, with context/limits. Avoid claims of reduced loneliness, employment success, conversion or retention uplift.
- Remove private data and unsupported quotes/metrics. Review consent for any future research material before publishing.

A useful interview walkthrough: explain the original goal, one difficult tradeoff, what evidence informed it, how the output was checked, what went wrong, and what you would test next. The objective is to demonstrate reasoning and learning, not inflate the project's maturity.
