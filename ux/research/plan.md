# Proposed research plan — local prototype

Status: NOT RUN. This is a plan, not a completed study or a promise of a particular sample-size coverage.

## Objective

Evaluate whether participants understand the purpose, light model, local data boundary and create/edit/recovery interactions. Identify usability problems before interpreting broader product outcomes. Do not test whether the product treats loneliness or helps people find jobs.

## Participants and method

Recruit a small initial round with relevant experience and varied devices/input needs, including keyboard and assistive-technology perspectives. Agree the actual number and inclusion criteria before recruitment. No recruitment, contact, interviews or recording has taken place through these files.

Use moderated task observation with a neutral introduction and fictional supplied story data. Explain that this is browser-local and labelled samples are not other live participants. Ask participants to think aloud if comfortable; avoid coaching until a task is recorded as assisted. A facilitator should not treat their own expectations as findings.

## Tasks and observations

| Task | Ask / action | Observe |
|---|---|---|
| U-01 | Explain what the page is for and what a light means. | Comprehension, sample/real confusion and questions. |
| U-02 | Find a province/city and equivalent map information. | Discovery, keyboard/dropdown/text use and interpretation of counts. |
| U-03 | Create a local candle with the fictional nickname/city/message. | Required/optional understanding, privacy/local boundary and completion. |
| U-04 | Enter an invalid city, recover, then cancel a changed story without losing the draft. | Error comprehension, focus, retry and discard decision. |
| U-05 | Toggle the candle, change location and hide the story. | Whether employment status is incorrectly assumed to change, and whether the display matches the participant's expectation. |
| U-06 | Reload, then explain what happens in another browser/device. | Persistence understanding; do not imply sync that is absent. |
| U-07 | Use keyboard/enlarged text or the participant's usual access tools. | Focus visibility/order, equivalent content and any loss of operation. |

Online signup/encouragement tasks are excluded from the active study until explicitly activated and verified. A future concept test can discuss a labelled proposed flow, but cannot claim real message delivery.

## Data and reporting

Use the blank session template. Obtain informed consent for the agreed observation/recording; participation is voluntary and can stop. Avoid collecting real employment, health, email or identifying story details. Keep any raw consent/recording in an appropriately controlled location, not this public repository. Publish only anonymized observations/aggregates with permission; distinguish observed facts, interpretation and proposed changes.

Record attempted/completed/assisted tasks, errors, elapsed time where appropriate and participant comments. Report sample/context/limitations. Do not manufacture findings or a conversion/retention uplift. Iterate after the first genuine round and retest the affected golden requirements.
