# Assumptions and discovery questions

Status: hypotheses, not validated personas or interview findings.

| Assumption / need segment | Basis | Question to test |
|---|---|---|
| A job seeker may prefer low-pressure participation and a nickname. | Product intent and stakeholder direction. | Can a participant understand what is required, optional, visible and local without assistance? |
| A visitor may understand geographic lights as community presence. | Chosen map/night-light concept. | Do they confuse light intensity with population, verified jobs or individual candle placement? |
| Someone wishing to encourage another person may prefer a simple supportive action. | Chosen I'm with you interaction; online delivery deferred. | Can they explain what the action would send and who could see it? |
| Keyboard/nonvisual users need equivalent map information. | Accessibility requirements and implemented text/dropdown alternatives. | Can they find the same location/count information without hover or visual brightness? |
| Clear local/online wording helps establish trust. | Actual owner defect report about unusable accounts and misleading readiness. | Can a participant predict whether their candle will appear on another device or to another person? |

These segments are not invented named personas with fictional biographies presented as research. Record real observations only after consent and execution; distinguish stakeholder feedback from participant evidence. The owner is a source of product feedback, not a representative sample of the intended audience.
